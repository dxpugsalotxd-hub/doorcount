Simple moving checklist

Moving goes wrong in the last week when everything is left to the end. Spread it out.

8 weeks before

  • Book movers or a truck. Good dates go fast at the end of the month.
  • Start getting rid of things you won't take: sell, donate or throw away.
  • Collect boxes, tape and markers.

4 weeks before

  • Start packing rooms you don't use daily: guest room, storage, books, seasonal clothes.
  • Number every box and list what's inside as you pack. Here's a labeling system that works.
  • Change your address with the post office, bank, work and subscriptions.
  • Book internet setup at the new place.

1 week before

  • Pack everything except daily basics.
  • Confirm the movers, the time and both addresses.
  • Arrange utilities to switch off at the old place and on at the new one.
  • Take move-out photos of your rental, room by room.

The day before

  • Pack your open-first box: phone chargers, toilet paper, towels, bedding, a kettle or coffee maker, mugs, basic tools, medicine, snacks and cleaning supplies.
  • Keep documents, keys, cash and valuables in a bag that stays with you.
  • Defrost the fridge if it's moving.

Moving day

  • Walk the old place one last time: closets, cabinets, behind doors.
  • Tell helpers which color goes to which room.
  • Check boxes off as they come in.
