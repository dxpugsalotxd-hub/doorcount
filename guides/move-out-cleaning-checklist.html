Move-out cleaning checklist

Cleaning is the most common reason landlords keep part of a deposit. Go room by room and take photos when you're done.

Kitchen

  • Inside the oven, the stovetop and under the burner plates
  • Inside the fridge and freezer (empty, defrosted, wiped, door left slightly open if it's switched off)
  • Microwave inside and out
  • Cabinets and drawers, inside and out
  • Sink, faucet and the drain
  • Backsplash and the wall behind the stove

Bathroom

  • Toilet, including the base and behind it
  • Tub or shower, tiles and grout, and the shower door track
  • Sink, mirror and cabinet
  • Exhaust fan cover

Every room

  • Floors swept, mopped or vacuumed, including corners and under where furniture stood
  • Baseboards, light switches, door handles and door frames
  • Windows, sills and blinds
  • Closets empty, shelves wiped
  • Nail holes filled if your lease asks for it
  • Light bulbs working

Normal wear and tear vs damage

Usually wear and tear (not your cost): faded paint, light carpet wear in walkways, small scuffs, loose door handles from normal use.

Usually damage (can be deducted): large holes in walls, burns or big stains on carpet, broken blinds, pet damage, unapproved paint colors.
