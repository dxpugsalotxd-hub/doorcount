What a good resume needs

  • One page if you have under about 10 years of experience
  • A simple layout that applicant tracking systems (ATS) can read: no tables, text boxes or photos
  • Bullet points that start with a verb and include a number where you can ("Cut wait times by 20%")
  • A short skills section using the same words as the job post

Before you send it

  • Save it as a PDF named like FirstName-LastName-Resume.pdf
  • Read it out loud once to catch typos
  • Tweak the top summary and skills for each job you apply to
