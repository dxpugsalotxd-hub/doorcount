How to make a QR code for Google reviews

Most happy customers would leave a review if it took one tap. A QR code at your counter makes it one tap.

1. Get your Google review link

On your phone, search your business name on Google while signed in to the account that manages it. Tap Ask for reviews (sometimes called Get more reviews) and copy the link. It usually starts with g.page/r/.

No access to the owner account? You can use your business's Place ID from Google's free Place ID finder. The review link is https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid= followed by that ID.

2. Turn it into a printable sign

Paste the link into Rave. It makes the QR code and puts it on a ready-to-print counter sign with your business name, five stars and a short line like "Loved your visit? Leave us a review!" Tap Test my link first to check it opens your review page.

3. Put it where people wait

  • By the register or card reader
  • On tables, mirrors or the waiting room wall
  • On receipts, invoices and job folders (small handout cards work well)

4. Ask the people who already left

A short text the same day gets the most replies: "Hi Sarah, thanks for coming in today! If you have a minute, a quick Google review helps us a lot:" plus your link. Rave has this message and six others ready to copy.

Rules worth knowing

  • Ask everyone, not only happy customers. Google doesn't allow screening out unhappy ones.
  • Don't offer discounts or gifts for reviews. Google can remove those reviews.
  • Reply to every review, good or bad. Future customers read the replies.
