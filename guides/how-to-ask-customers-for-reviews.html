How to ask customers for a Google review

Most customers are happy to leave a review. They just need to be asked at the right moment, with a link that takes one tap.

When to ask

  • Right after a good visit, while they're still at the counter or walking out.
  • The same day by text, if you have their number. Replies drop off fast after a day or two.
  • After a finished job for trades and home services, once the customer has seen the result.

Text message example

Hi Sarah, thanks for choosing Noor's Barbershop today! If you have a minute, a quick Google review would help us a lot: [your link]

Email example

Subject: How did we do, Sarah? Hi Sarah, Thank you for choosing Noor's Barbershop. We hope everything went well. Would you share a few words about your experience on Google? It takes about 30 seconds, and it helps other people in the area find us: [your link] Thank you, Omar

A friendly reminder, a few days later

Hi Sarah, just a quick reminder from Noor's Barbershop. If you get a moment, we'd really appreciate your review here: [your link] No worries if not, and thanks again!

What not to do

  • Don't offer a discount, gift or entry into a draw for a review. Google can remove those reviews.
  • Don't only ask happy customers. Google doesn't allow screening out unhappy ones.
  • Don't send more than one reminder.
