How do I find my review link?
  1. Easiest: on your phone, open Google and search your business name while signed in to the Google account that owns it. Tap Ask for reviews (or Get more reviews), then Copy link, and paste it above.
  2. Or: open Google's Place ID finder, type your business name, copy the code that starts with ChIJ, and paste it above.
  3. Not sure? Leave it empty and add your city below. Your sign will open your business on Google Maps, where customers tap Reviews.
Using Yelp, Facebook or another site instead? Paste that review page link. It works the same way.

Ask by text or email

Send these from your own phone to customers you just served. Add their first name so it feels personal.

Good to know

  • Ask every customer, not only the happy ones. Google doesn't allow screening out unhappy customers, and honest reviews build more trust anyway.
  • Don't offer discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews. Google can remove those reviews.
  • The best time to ask is right after a good visit or a finished job, while they're still smiling.
